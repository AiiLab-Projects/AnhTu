Vietnamese voice recordings for Cô Tú

Generated with Piper's vi_VN-vais1000-medium voice:
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/vi/vi_VN/vais1000/medium
Model repository license: MIT.

The voice was trained on the VAIS-1000 Vietnamese Speech Synthesis Corpus:
https://ieee-dataport.org/documents/vais-1000-vietnamese-speech-synthesis-corpus
Dataset license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0):

These recordings were generated from the Cô Tú site's lesson and exercise text.
The audio clips are adaptations produced for this website, not recordings by
Nguyễn Anh Tú. The model and dataset creators do not endorse this website.

Additional voices (subfolders hn-ngochuyen, hue-huonggiang, sg-thaotrinh,
sg-minhhoang) were generated with Vbee AIVoice (https://vbee.vn) using the
voices HN - Ngọc Huyền, Huế - Hương Giang, SG - Thảo Trinh and SG - Minh Hoàng,
under the site owner's Vbee account. They are synthetic voices, not recordings
of Nguyễn Anh Tú or of the voice actors.
